Sensitivity Analysis — Net GP Contribution at Varying BSP Job Volumes
BSP Volume Total Jobs Utilization BSP Revenue Net GP Contribution Annual Impact Status
Methodology: Net GP contribution = total portfolio GP uplift (post-BSP minus pre-BSP) minus monthly marketing spend. Cannibalization deducts the full standard GP foregone on shifted jobs. Break-even (profitability basis) = Marketing cost ÷ Net BSP GP per job after cannibalization drag — at base case this is ~174 jobs/month. Upsell excluded: Inspection-driven repair conversion (KWD 2,700/month est. at 30% base-case conversion rate) is excluded from all figures above and treated as incremental upside. No incremental infrastructure investment required during the pilot phase.
